Legal

Terms & Policies

Last Updated: January 2025

Order Policy

All orders placed through St. Veronica Garments are subject to review and confirmation. Submission of an order form does not constitute a binding contract until confirmed in writing by our team.

  • A minimum order of 12 garments is required per order.
  • Rush orders (needed within 7 days) may incur additional fees.
  • Orders are confirmed only after design approval and deposit payment.
  • Changes to order quantities or design must be submitted at least 5 business days before the print date.

Design & File Requirements

To ensure the highest print quality, all submitted designs must meet the following requirements:

  • Files must be submitted in .SVG or .AI format with all text converted to outlines/curves.
  • Designs must be provided at a minimum of 300 DPI equivalent resolution.
  • St. Veronica Garments is not responsible for poor print quality resulting from low-resolution or improperly formatted files.
  • By submitting a design, you confirm you hold the rights or license to use that design for printing.

Payment Policy

  • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required before production begins.
  • The remaining balance is due upon pickup or before shipment.
  • We accept cash, check (made out to St. Veronica Garments), and digital payments via Zelle or CashApp.

Returns & Reprints

Due to the custom nature of screen printed garments, all sales are final. However:

  • If an error was made on our part (wrong color, wrong placement, misprinted design), we will reprint the affected garments at no charge.
  • Claims for errors must be submitted within 5 business days of receiving your order.
  • Garments must be unworn and unwashed to qualify for a reprint claim.
  • We are not responsible for sizing errors if the correct sizes were ordered and printed.

Turnaround Time

  • Standard orders: 10–14 business days from design approval and deposit.
  • Rush orders: 5–7 business days (subject to availability and additional fee).
  • Turnaround times begin after full design approval, not order submission.

Privacy Policy

St. Veronica Garments collects personal information (name, email, phone number) solely for the purpose of processing and fulfilling your order. We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Your information may be used to contact you regarding your order status or future ministry opportunities with our team.

Ministry Mission

St. Veronica Garments is a faith-based screen printing ministry. We reserve the right to decline orders that conflict with our Christian values and beliefs. We are committed to serving all faith communities with excellence, integrity, and love.
