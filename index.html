Terms and policies

How orders are handled

Artwork

Vector artwork is preferred. Please upload `.svg` or `.ai` files when submitting your request. Low-resolution files may need cleanup before printing.

Timeline

Order timelines depend on garment availability, design approval, and the print schedule. Rush orders may not always be possible.

Approval

Printing begins after garment details, artwork placement, quantity, pricing, and pickup details are confirmed.

Changes

Changes after approval may affect completion date and final cost. Reprints are reviewed case by case.
